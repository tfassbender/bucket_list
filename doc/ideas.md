# Feature ideas

A collection of ideas, **not a specification**. Nothing here is detailed enough to implement yet. When
an idea is refined, it moves into [design.md](design.md) or its own document.

## Platform

- Must work on **desktop and mobile**, but **optimized for mobile** (phone-sized screens, touch input,
  quick capture on the go).

## Bucket lists

- Create, rename and delete bucket lists.
- Add **details to a list**, for example a description, notes, or maybe a cover image.

## Items

- **Add** items to a list and **remove** them.
- **Reorder** items (drag and drop, or move up/down on touch devices).
- **Check items off** (mark as done) and un-check them again.
- Add **details to an item**, for example notes, links, a target date, tags, or a location.

## Images

- Attach **images** to items (and maybe to lists), for example inspiration photos beforehand or
  memories once something is done.
- To clarify later: storage location and size limits, resizing/thumbnails, and uploading from a phone camera.

## Collaboration

- Work together with a partner on shared lists (see [accounts_and_sharing.md](accounts_and_sharing.md)).
- See who added, changed or checked off what.

## Maybe later

- Photo "memories" or a short note when completing an item.
- Comments on items.
- An activity feed per shared space.
- Picking a random open item ("what do we do this weekend?").
- Installable as a PWA on the phone's home screen.
- Estimated cost, priority, map view.
